About us

Building families that stand on their own

A food parcel feeds a family for a week. Then they wait for the next one. Tamkeen starts with what a family needs right now and stays until they can stand on their own.

Every family we reach fills in a short survey with a volunteer, so we understand their income, skills and goals and can plan the right support together.

Our vision is a Pakistan where every family earns its living with dignity, and no one depends on charity to survive.

What we do

Four ways we help a family

Relief keeps a family safe this month. The other three make sure they won’t need relief next year.

  • Essentials when they’re needed most

    Monthly groceries, warm blankets and shoes for verified families.

  • A survey and a personal plan

    Two volunteers visit, listen and agree next steps with the family.

  • Skills and tools

    Training in a trade, plus the machine, cart or stock to start working.

  • Interest-free loans

    Qarz-e-Hasna, repaid in small instalments that fund the next family.

How it works

From need to independence, step by step

  1. Relief
  2. Listen
  3. Build
  4. Stand tall
  1. 1

    Step 1: Relief

    Groceries, warm blankets and shoes, so the family is fed and warm and can think beyond today.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Listen

    A volunteer visits and fills in a short survey with the family: their income, skills and goals. Together they agree a personal plan.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Build

    Skills training, tools or a small interest-free loan, such as a sewing machine, a food cart or livestock, with mentoring along the way.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Stand tall

    A steady income of their own. Our support ends, and the repaid loan helps the next family begin.

Questions

Questions? Here are straight answers

Something else on your mind? Message us on WhatsApp and a team member will reply.

How do you choose which families to help?

Families are referred by people the community trusts, such as an imam, a teacher or a local councillor. Two volunteers visit the home and fill in a short survey, and a committee approves support using written criteria, never personal connections.

Do you give cash?

For relief we give goods: groceries, blankets and shoes. For livelihoods we provide tools, stock or training, or a small interest-free loan that the family repays in instalments to fund the next family.

Can I choose where my donation goes?

Yes. Choose a purpose in the donation form, such as a winter kit, monthly groceries, skills training or a livelihood start. If you choose “wherever it’s needed most”, we use it where the gap is largest.

Will I get a receipt?

Yes. After you send your donation details, we confirm the transfer and send a receipt to your WhatsApp or email.

Who can volunteer?

Anyone who will treat families with respect and keep their information private. Home visits are done in pairs, and we’ll brief you before your first day.
